0. THE BIG PICTURE  [YOURS]
--------------------------
  - Dragon Wish is the online game, with its own story set in the present. This story game is
    its PREQUEL and ties into Dragon Wish's present.
  - You play a Saiyan (more to come), the student of Kohaku Genzo.


1. THE CORE: THE DEMON PEARLS  [YOURS]
-------------------------------------
  - Long before time itself had a name, the supreme Demon Kings reigned. They held the power
    of multiple universes.
  - The one known as THE PAST RULER led his army in a billion-year-long war against them.
  - He finally led them into a clever trap. His plan: spend all of his life force, which would
    entirely destroy him and all his essence, to destroy the Demon Kings.
  - The kings were too powerful. Destroying them failed. He tried to seal them, and even that
    wasn't enough.
  - His last option: turn the demons into PEARLS, 10 for each king (30 in all), which scattered
    across that universe. The sealed demons blasted off in different directions as the Past
    Ruler faded.
  - His army came and drained all their power into him. He explained that he had used more
    than he was even allowed to: everything he was, plus a BINDING DEAL that would completely
    erase him in return for two times the power he had.
  - The army's energy only very briefly kept him around. They argued he could live on the
    energy they gave him. He screamed that they were fools with no understanding of a binding
    deal: such a great ask comes with a great punishment. If he tried to escape total death,
    the demons' binding would come undone.
  - So he used the last of that energy to put a BARRIER around the 30 pearls. It would kill
    anyone who dared to touch one. The army argued the barrier was harsh; the Ruler said the
    pearls must be feared for the rest of history.
  - He said the energy in the pearls would slowly disperse into the universe over billions of
    years, until the pearls were no longer physical and the energy had become natural, fresh
    life energy, mixed into the universe's own energy and diluted.
  - Billions of years later, the story of the battle has been lost to history. And the Ruler
    MISCALCULATED: he was too drained and dazed, and the barrier finally disappeared.
  - THE BARRIER FADED SLOWLY (retcon): it started fading about 400 years before Vex found his
    pearl. From then on it only killed those who weren't strong enough. It vanished completely
    10 years before Vex found his pearl (Kazgor felt the jolt; Vex worked it out on paper).

4. THE RULES OF THE PEARLS
--------------------------
  Decided [YOURS]:
    - A pearl speaks to you when you're close, and its king can look into your mind.
    - The kings are weaker than they were, but still very powerful.
    - Eating a pearl gives you strength beyond your wildest dreams. The first pearl is only a
      boost in power.
    - One pearl is too weak for its king to take control of you: if he tried, the burst of
      power would destroy the pearl, and you with it.
    - How many pearls before a king can take control? Even Vex and Kazgor are unsure.
    - One pearl is roughly 10% of its king (a much weaker version now, but still very, very
      powerful): one pearl made Vex king of Earth.
    - Holding a pearl (the Kohaku pearl, from 1653 on) leaves a strong fighter weak as a
      newborn; putting it down gives the strength back. Worn against the skin, it's a strength
      restrictor; in a pocket, it isn't.
    - A swallowed pearl takes a moment to take hold: punched out of Vex before then, it was
      lost to him.
  [OPEN]
    - Can one person eat pearls from different kings?
    - Is a king one mind spread over his ten pearls? Can he feel his other pearls?
    - Can a pearl be destroyed? Can the kings be sealed again, and at what cost?
    - The restriction after the barrier vanished: see note A in section 10.


5. VEXTRILL ("VEX"), THE GAME'S FIRST FINAL VILLAIN  [YOURS]
-----------------------------------------------------------
  - A billionaire, in charge of the fight tournaments. Very intelligent; has studied books ever
    since he was a child. Art: masked, a visor, an arm cannon (red and orange).
  - His favourite book, passed down his family for generations, he believed was fiction: the
    legend of the Demon Kings.
  - He dreamed of being a ruler like the supreme kings, and believed the Demon Kings were the
    good ones, keeping strict order:
      "The demon kings' powers were that of multiple gods. They could erase an entire galaxy
      with a thought. This brought peace according to their rule, and in the case of a law
      breaker, they were entirely erased, no problems."
    He justified their evils as "natural": "with such power, naturally you would be more than
    just bored."
  - A pearl was put on show at a museum, at an absurd price, for being deadly to touch and for
    the immense power flowing from it. Its sellers didn't know the barrier had faded.
  - Exactly as the book described. Vex tried to tell himself the book was written about the
    pearl. It bothered him; the next week he drove to the museum and studied the pearl.
  - Kazgor looked into his mind, a little excited at the 0.00001% chance this human knew. He
    did. Kazgor told him everything was true, he is the Ember King, and Vex must find a way to
    consume him.
  - Vex has read the book thousands of times and can recite at least 90% of it; he spent a summer
    working out every soldier's power: the barrier must have lifted at least 10 years ago.
  - Kazgor: about 10 years ago a sudden jolt of power woke him. But Vex may be the only one in
    the universe, if not the multiverse, who knows the legend; it's too risky for him to die.
  - Vex: "You've been in this pearl for billions of years, Lord Kazgor. The battle you fought is
    faded at best. I've studied it for years. You must trust me." He walks away.
  - Auction day: Vex spends almost everything he has and easily wins it; everyone is scared.
  - At home he grabs the pearl. Nothing happens. He swallows it.
    KAZGOR: "YES! YES! Well done, my child! Your body is a suitable vessel. But this one pearl
    is far too weak. If I attempt to control you, the power burst will destroy this pearl and
    you."
  - By 2043, a hundred years later, Vex is the KING OF EARTH. One pearl alone ruled over humans.
  - He leveled the Kohaku empire, your master's. He knows the family has a pearl.


6. THE KOHAKU CLAN AND KOHAKU GENZO  [YOURS]
-------------------------------------------
  - 1653: the king of the SHIRANUI CLAN, its pride, far beyond the title "Ki Master". Nobody
    could challenge him; no nation would even try. Everyone thought this was good, but he had
    trained his whole life for this power out of love of battle, and that power is what ended
    his exciting battles. Life was boring. He had a son, but he was selfish for battle.
  - He heard of a pearl that killed anyone who touched it. One random night he went to it and
    held it. Instantly he felt paralysed; his strength was like a newborn baby's. He couldn't
    even get the pearl out of his palms. That night he crawled for miles.
  - Hours later they found him dirty, scratched up, covered in insect bites, still holding the
    pearl. They all freaked out, but he was the most pumped he had ever been: "like drinking
    pure mountain water after being thirsty all your life". He shakily put the pearl down and
    got his strength back.
  - That day he renamed the clan THE KOHAKU CLAN.
  - He trained with the pearl. He trained his son to the brink of death some days, until his
    power was just enough not to die, then trained him even harder with the pearl. From when
    his son was 16 they sparred constantly, and he found his love of battle again.
  - The pearl is passed from son to son as a strength restrictor.
  - KOHAKU GENZO, your master, holds it now (worn on a cord around his neck). His motto:
    "You must train till you die. Adapt to death and TRAIN HARDER!!"
  - The Kohaku were once a powerful empire, leveled by Vex. Now you live in a poor, isolated
    area.
  [SUGGESTED] notes:
    - "Kohaku" means amber in Japanese: the stone famous for keeping things trapped inside it
      for millions of years. "Shiranui" means "unknown fire" (ghost lights over the sea). The
      clan went from a mystery fire to a thing sealed in stone.
    - Genzo's method is accidentally perfect for a Saiyan: Saiyans come back stronger from
      near-death (zenkai). He may not know why his student grows so fast.

8. THE END OF THE FIRST SAGA  [YOURS]
------------------------------------
  (A lot happens before this; you'll write it later.)
  - You fight Vex and are close to death. Vex knows your master has the pearl and has been
    tossing you around to make you tell him. "Fine, I'll find out for myself": he charges a beam
    to kill you.
  - A rock is thrown at Vex. He looks up: Genzo.
  - Fighting. Genzo takes off the pearl and puts it in his pocket, freeing him.
  - Later, Vex and Genzo are both badly hurt. Vex gets a lucky shot and holds Genzo by the neck.
    You try to get up and stop him, but Vex puts a hole straight through Genzo, and takes the
    pearl. He marvels at it and starts to monologue.
  - You shake. You grind your teeth until it looks like sawdust. You bite your lip and it bursts
    and bleeds. Fists clenched, incoherent, you muster: "I... WILL... KILL... YOU."
  - You scream. A heavy flash of yellow covers the scene: your hair is different, and so is your
    power level. (Super Saiyan.)
  - Vex swallows the pearl. You dash in and gut punch him; he chokes on it; you punch him in the
    throat to make him cough it out. You grab it: "I'm sorry, master."
  - You beat him down and rip him in two. He crawls; you follow and see him searching the dirt.
    He eats something. You check your pocket: you must have dropped it.
  - He's too distracted to fight properly. You charge a beam and blast him, leaving a crater.
  - For the player, he's dead. He isn't.
  - Why it's built this way: it took a whole fight, your master's life, and you in SSJ while Vex
    was distracted to bring him down. One pearl is technically 10% of a supreme Demon King (a
    much weaker version, but still very, very powerful). Beating him while mostly healthy would
    scale SSJ too far.


9. DRAFT SCENES  [SUGGESTED: your story, written as game scenes]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  OPENING NARRATION
    Before time had a name, there were the Demon Kings.
    Kazgor, the Ember King. Zarveth, the Crimson King. Nemryth, the Void King.
    They held the power of many universes, and they ruled them all.

    One rose against them: the one we now call the Past Ruler.
    For a billion years his army fought theirs. For a billion years, neither side could win.

    So he set a trap.

    He meant to burn every drop of his life to destroy them. It was not enough.
    He tried to seal them away. It was not enough.
    So he did the last thing he could: he broke them. Each king was crushed into ten pearls,
    and thirty pearls scattered across the universe like falling stars.

    To do it, he took more than he was allowed. He made a binding deal: everything he was,
    his very existence, in return for twice his power. And when the light faded, the deal came
    to collect.

    His army ran to him and poured their own strength into his fading body. "Live," they
    begged.
    "Fools!" he screamed. "A deal this great carries a punishment just as great. If I escape
    my death, the binding breaks, and they come back."

    So he spent their gift on one last act: a barrier around every pearl, that would kill
    anything that touched it.
    "It's too cruel," they said.
    "They must be feared," he answered, "for the rest of history."

    In time, he told them, the pearls would melt away. Their power would seep into the
    universe, thin out, and become nothing more than the quiet energy of life.

    Then he was gone.

    Billions of years passed. The war was forgotten. The Past Ruler was forgotten.
    But he had been drained, and dazed, and dying, and he had made one mistake.

    The barrier did not last as long as the pearls.

10. NOTES  [SUGGESTED]
---------------------
  A. THE RESTRICTOR AFTER THE BARRIER VANISHED. In 1653 the fading barrier explains why the
     pearl didn't kill the Shiranui king. But the barrier vanished completely ~1933, so why does
     the pearl still restrict Genzo in 2043? Options:
       1. (my favourite) The weakening was never the barrier: a sealed king drinks the strength
          of whoever holds him, and the stronger you are, the more he drinks. That explains the
          1653 king collapsing, the restrictor still working in 2043, AND why Vex (a bookworm
          with no fighting power) felt nothing when he grabbed his pearl. Dark bonus: the Kohaku
          clan has been feeding their pearl for 390 years, so instead of fading like the others
          it's been topped up: the strongest of the thirty. And a beat: when you grab it in SSJ
          at the end, your power stutters until it's in your pocket.
       2. A last remnant of the barrier lives on in that one pearl, kept alive by the clan's
          training against it.
  B. AGE RATING (if it's on Roblox): a hole through someone's chest and ripping someone in two
     count as impalement and dismemberment. If they look realistic the game is 18+ only (age
     verified); shown Dragon Ball style (a flash, a cut-away, a silhouette), it stays open to
     everyone.
  C. THE POCKET ECHO: Genzo pockets the pearl to fight at full strength; you pocket it and lose
     it. Same act, opposite result.
  D. WHAT DISTRACTED VEX AT THE END depends on whose pearl it was: if it's Kazgor's second, maybe
     Kazgor tries to seize control for the first time; if it's a brother's, two kings are
     fighting over his body. Either way, Vex now carries two pearls.
  E. Earlier ideas still open: the book written by one of the Past Ruler's soldiers (Vex
     descends from the army that sealed the kings); the tournaments as Vex's net (and a bridge
     to Dragon Wish's tournaments); a pearl radar; the pearls being partly dissolved into all
     life energy; "the Past Ruler" as the ruler of the past itself; the other side of the
     binding deal.
