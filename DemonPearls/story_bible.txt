1. THE CORE: THE DEMON PEARLS  [YOURS]
-------------------------------------
  - Long before time itself had a name, the supreme Demon Kings reigned. They held the power
    of multiple universes.
  - The one known as THE PAST RULER led his army in a billion-year-long war against them.
  - He finally led them into a clever trap. His plan: spend all of his life force, which would
    entirely destroy him and all his essence, to destroy the Demon Kings.
  - The kings were too powerful. Destroying them failed. He tried to seal them, and even that
    wasn't enough.
  - His last option: turn the demons into PEARLS, 10 for each king (30 in all), which scattered
    across that universe. The sealed demons blasted off in different directions as the Past
    Ruler faded.
  - His army came and drained all their power into him. He explained that he had used more
    than he was even allowed to: everything he was, plus a BINDING DEAL that would completely
    erase him in return for two times the power he had.
  - The army's energy only very briefly kept him around. They argued he could live on the
    energy they gave him. He screamed that they were fools with no understanding of a binding
    deal: such a great ask comes with a great punishment. If he tried to escape total death,
    the demons' binding would come undone.
  - So he used the last of that energy to put a BARRIER around the 30 pearls. It would kill
    anyone who dared to touch one. The army argued the barrier was harsh; the Ruler said the
    pearls must be feared for the rest of history.
  - He said the energy in the pearls would slowly disperse into the universe over billions of
    years, until the pearls were no longer physical and the energy had become natural, fresh
    life energy, mixed into the universe's own energy and diluted.
  - Billions of years later, the story of the battle has been lost to history. And, most
    importantly, the Ruler MISCALCULATED: he was too drained and dazed, and the barrier around
    the pearls has finally disappeared. It fell about 10 years before the story starts, and
    nobody noticed (Kazgor felt a jolt of power that woke him; Vex worked it out on paper).


2. THE THREE DEMON KINGS  [YOURS]
--------------------------------
  ZARVETH, THE CRIMSON KING: the eldest and the strongest. Red, the giant in the middle of the
    art, crowned, huge horns, a cape, under a blood-red moon.
  KAZGOR, THE EMBER KING: Zarveth's younger brother. Orange, molten cracks through a rocky body,
    crescent horns, a jagged grin. The first king to wake and find a vessel (Vex).
  NEMRYTH, THE VOID KING: Zarveth's younger brother. Black, a ragged shadow cloak covered in
    eyes, a pale sigil above him.
  Each has a sigil under his name in the art.

  [SUGGESTED] brothers: Kazgor making a deal on his own may be trying to get back first, before

3. THE RULES OF THE PEARLS
--------------------------
  Decided [YOURS]:
    - A pearl speaks to you when you're close, and its king can look into your mind.
    - The kings are weaker than they were, but still very powerful.
    - Eating a pearl gives you strength beyond your wildest dreams.
    - One pearl is too weak for its king to take control of you: if he tried, the burst of
      power would destroy the pearl, and you with it.
  [OPEN]
    - How many pearls before a king can take control? Does Vex know?
    - Does each king's pearl give its own kind of power? Can one person eat pearls from
      different kings?
    - Is a king one mind spread over his ten pearls, or ten minds? Can he feel where his other
      pearls are?
    - Can a pearl be destroyed? Can the kings be sealed again, and at what cost?


4. VEXTRILL ("VEX"), THE GAME'S FIRST FINAL VILLAIN  [YOURS]
-----------------------------------------------------------
  - A billionaire, in charge of the fight tournaments. Very intelligent; has studied books ever
    since he was a child.
  - His favourite book, passed down his family for generations, he believed was fiction: the
    legend of the Demon Kings.
  - He dreamed of being a ruler like the supreme kings, and believed the Demon Kings were the
    good ones, keeping strict order:
      "The demon kings' powers were that of multiple gods. They could erase an entire galaxy
      with a thought. This brought peace according to their rule, and in the case of a law
      breaker, they were entirely erased, no problems."
    He justified their evils as "natural": "with such power, naturally you would be more than
    just bored."
  - Years later a pearl was put on show at a museum, at an absurd price, for being deadly to
    touch and for the immense power flowing from it. The people selling it had no idea its
    barrier had faded; they only went off the legend.
  - It was exactly as the book described. Vex knew he had to have it. He tried to tell himself
    the book was fiction, probably written about the pearl and not as old as it claims. It
    bothered him. The next week he drove to the museum and studied the pearl.
  - Kazgor, curious why this man was inspecting him like that, looked into his mind, a little
    excited at the 0.00001% chance this human knew. He did. Kazgor spoke to him telepathically:
    everything in the book is true, he is the Ember King, and Vex must find a way to consume him.
  - Vex: he has read that book thousands of times and can recite at least 90% of it from memory;
    he has had hundreds of theories and done more than enough research. He spent a summer
    working out how much power every individual soldier would have had, and the barrier must
    have lifted years ago, at least 10, and nobody knows.
  - Kazgor hesitated: about 10 years ago he was woken by a sudden jolt of power, but paid it no
    attention as he couldn't do anything. Still, Vex is most likely the only one in the
    universe, if not the multiverse, who knows the legend; it's too risky for him to die.
  - Vex: "You've been in this pearl for billions of years, Lord Kazgor. The battle you fought is
    faded at best. I've studied it for years. You must trust me." He walks away.
  - Auction day: Vex spends almost everything he has and easily wins the pearl, because everyone
    else is scared of it.
  - At home he opens the case and grabs the pearl. Nothing happens. He swallows it.
    KAZGOR: "YES! YES! Well done, my child! Your body is a suitable vessel. But this one pearl
    is far too weak. If I attempt to control you, the power burst will destroy this pearl and
    you."
  - Vex now has new power.

  [SUGGESTED] ideas around Vex:
    - EVERY PEARL BRINGS HIM CLOSER TO HIS DOOM: more pearls, more power, but more of Kazgor
      inside him, until Kazgor can take control. His ambition is the trap. Being a genius, he'd
      see it coming and try to cheat it, e.g. eat pearls from different kings so no one brother
      ever gets enough of a foothold: the brothers' rivalry keeping each other in check inside
      his body.
    - WHO WROTE THE BOOK: it's too accurate to be fiction, down to the soldiers' power. A
      soldier of the Past Ruler's army wrote it, and it was copied down Vex's family for ages:
      Vex descends from the army that sealed the kings. The bloodline meant to keep the pearls
      feared produced the one man who loves them.
    - THE TOURNAMENTS ARE HIS NET: nearly broke after the auction, he needs them to refill his
      fortune and to hunt pearls (fighters as unwitting pearl hunters, or a pearl as a prize
      nobody else understands). A natural bridge to Dragon Wish: its tournaments could be his.
    - A DEMON RADAR: with a pearl inside him he might feel Kazgor's other nine; a billionaire
      genius would build a machine around that feeling, his own dark Dragon Radar.
    - THE IRONY: the Past Ruler's last order was that the pearls be feared forever. The fear
      outlived the barrier, and it's exactly why nobody else bid. His protection handed the
      pearl to the one man who wasn't afraid.


5. DRAFT SCENES  [SUGGESTED: your story, written as game scenes]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  OPENING NARRATION
    Before time had a name, there were the Demon Kings.
    Kazgor, the Ember King. Zarveth, the Crimson King. Nemryth, the Void King.
    They held the power of many universes, and they ruled them all.

    One rose against them: the one we now call the Past Ruler.
    For a billion years his army fought theirs. For a billion years, neither side could win.

    So he set a trap.

    He meant to burn every drop of his life to destroy them. It was not enough.
    He tried to seal them away. It was not enough.
    So he did the last thing he could: he broke them. Each king was crushed into ten pearls,
    and thirty pearls scattered across the universe like falling stars.

    To do it, he took more than he was allowed. He made a binding deal: everything he was,
    his very existence, in return for twice his power. And when the light faded, the deal came
    to collect.

    His army ran to him and poured their own strength into his fading body. "Live," they
    begged.
    "Fools!" he screamed. "A deal this great carries a punishment just as great. If I escape
    my death, the binding breaks, and they come back."

    So he spent their gift on one last act: a barrier around every pearl, that would kill
    anything that touched it.
    "It's too cruel," they said.
    "They must be feared," he answered, "for the rest of history."

    In time, he told them, the pearls would melt away. Their power would seep into the
    universe, thin out, and become nothing more than the quiet energy of life.

    Then he was gone.

    Billions of years passed. The war was forgotten. The Past Ruler was forgotten.
    But he had been drained, and dazed, and dying, and he had made one mistake.

    The barrier did not last as long as the pearls.

  THE MUSEUM (the pearl on show before its auction)
    [A glass case under spotlights. A sign: LETHAL ON CONTACT - DO NOT APPROACH. Visitors keep
     a wide circle around it. One man stands right at the glass, and has for a long time:
     VEXTRILL.]
    KAZGOR (to himself): He studies me like a scholar, not a tourist...
    [The pearl pulses. Kazgor slips into Vex's mind: pages, thousands of them. The same book,
     read over and over.]
    KAZGOR: ...He knows.
    KAZGOR (in Vex's head): Don't look away, human. That book you love... every word of it is
      true. I am Kazgor, the Ember King. Find a way to consume me.
    VEX (thinking back, calm): I've read that book thousands of times. I can recite nine-tenths
      of it from memory. I spent a summer working out how much power each of the Past Ruler's
      soldiers gave him. That barrier fell at least ten years ago. Nobody noticed.
    KAZGOR: ...Ten years ago, something jolted me awake. I could do nothing with it.
    KAZGOR: No. If you're wrong, you die, and you may be the only one in the universe who still
      knows. I won't risk you.
    VEX: You've been in that pearl for billions of years, Lord Kazgor. Your war is a faded story
      at best. I've studied it for years.
    [He turns and walks away.]
    VEX: You must trust me.

  THE AUCTION
    [The opening price is read out. The room goes quiet. Nobody raises a paddle; nobody wants
     to own death.]
    [One paddle goes up.]
    AUCTIONEER: ...Sold.
    [Vex has spent almost everything he owns.]

  VEX'S STUDY
    [The case opens with a hiss. His bare hand hovers over the pearl. For billions of years,
     everything that touched it died.]
    [He picks it up. Nothing happens.]
    [He looks at it for a long moment. Then he swallows it.]
    KAZGOR: YES! YES! Well done, my child! Your body is a suitable vessel... but one pearl is
      far too weak. If I tried to take control of you now, the burst would destroy this pearl,
      and you with it.
    [Embers crawl along Vex's veins. He starts to laugh.]


6. OTHER IDEAS  [SUGGESTED]
--------------------------
  - THE OPENING SCENE: for billions of years, everything that touched a pearl died. The story
    opens on the day someone touches one and lives (now: Vex, in his study).
  - THE PEARLS AREN'T FULL ANY MORE: part of every king has already dissolved into the
    universe's life energy. The kings come back weaker (why mortals stand a chance), and a
    revived king could pull his missing power back out of every living thing.
  - "THE PAST RULER": if he ruled the past itself, erasing him explains why history forgot.
  - THE ARMY'S FATE: they or their descendants became guardians who kept the fear alive after
    the story was lost (see "who wrote the book" above).
  - THE OTHER SIDE OF THE DEAL: whoever the binding deal was made with may still exist.
