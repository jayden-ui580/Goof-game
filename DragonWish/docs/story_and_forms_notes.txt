2. SUPER SAIYAN GOD - THE KALHALLA QUEST [YOURS]
-----------------------------------------------
  Why: you hate the canon ritual (five righteous Saiyans pour power into a sixth; they even
  used a baby). No work, no struggle. Instead, a sort of Valhalla, which fits Saiyans.

  1. Go to a specific place (a temple) and sit, days and nights (sped up for Roblox), until
     you are dying.
  2. If your Saiyan heart is pure and full of warrior's spirit, your soul crosses over to
     KALHALLA while your body barely clings on back on earth.
  3. In Kalhalla: a short story quest. You fight a couple of characters and do some
     training until you're proven a worthy Saiyan.
  4. They take you to the highest point of their world, THE (name [OPEN]) SHRINE: high like
     Snake Way, days of stairs.
  5. At the top, on a podium, a chalice filled with clear liquid with a slight rainbow tint.
     It is only filled if you are truly worthy and pure.
  6. You drink, and wake up back in your body: skinny and dried out. White trails circle
     you, you levitate, your body turns white and glows, the light bursts: red hair.
     You're SSJG.
  7. The hair fades, leaving a permanent red glow around you: the SSJG sign. From then on,
     going Super Saiyan means going full SSJG. Later, Mastered SSJG: the hair is always red.

  After the SSJG sign you can enter Kalhalla any time: dailies, training, etc. [YOURS]

  [SUGGESTED, not decided]
    - Name: "Kalhalla" (kale + Valhalla), in Toriyama's vegetable-pun style. You've started
      using it.
    - Tie the vigil to zenkai (Saiyans come back stronger from near-death).
    - Keep the canon myth as a half-remembered legend: the "five righteous Saiyans" are
      really Kalhalla's five trial masters (strength, endurance, mercy, loyalty, will). Each
      one you pass pours its light into the chalice; fail one (e.g. finishing off a beaten
      opponent in the mercy trial) and you climb all those stairs to a cup that's only
      four-fifths full.
    - Trial masters named after vegetables (Chard, Okran...); the way up as a giant plant
      stalk ("the Stalk of Heaven"); the drink "Godwater".
    - The vigil shouldn't be an idle timer: 2-3 minutes with a day/night timelapse, your body
      withering, something to do (a breathing rhythm; visions tempting you to quit).
    - Your body stays at the temple (others can see someone attempting the ascension); your
      soul wears a halo in Kalhalla.
    - Make the stairs a mechanic: gravity gets heavier every flight.
    - The wake-up should be quiet (silence, a heartbeat, the trails), the opposite of SSJ's
      explosion.


3. THE PEAK PATHS (WHY THE DAWN FORM EXISTS) [YOURS]
-------------------------------------------------
  In canon, Goku's and Vegeta's forms follow each other, then split at the peak: one went the
  way of the angels (Ultra Instinct, silver), the other the way of the destroyers (Ultra Ego,
  purple). Orange hair isn't taken by any Saiyan form, so this game's peak goes a third way:
  the Saiyan way.

  [SUGGESTED] why orange fits your lore: Super Saiyan is gold (the Saiyan's own rage), SSJG is
  red (power from beyond). Gold + red = orange: the god power and the Saiyan power fuse in your
  blood. Orange is also the colour of the Dragon Balls (and the game is called Dragon Wish).
  (Note: Orange Piccolo exists in canon, but he's a Namekian; orange Saiyan hair is free.)


4. SUPER SAIYAN DAWN - THE SAIYAN WAY [YOURS]
--------------------------------------------
  Requirement: Mastered SSJG.

  1. Meditate, then enter Kalhalla.
  2. Regrow your tail: talk to a certain Saiyan there to get it back.
  3. Look into a full moon: you transform into an Oozaru TWICE the size of normal ones,
     with glowing red fur.
  4. Learn to control your rage. Easier if you finished an Oozaru-control side quest earlier
     in your playthrough.
  5. Sit and meditate. The task: expel every drop of ki out of you, then hold it around your
     whole body as a thin, controlled veil, without the ki shooting out, breaking things or
     blowing up the ground.
  6. Hold it until morning. When the sun is at its peak above you, you transform out of the
     Oozaru and glow incredibly bright (a far shot of the Earth for exaggeration).
  7. Your hair is spiky and looks just like SSJ, but calm and glowing orange.
  8. From now on the black base hair is gone: you permanently have non-glowing orange hair.
  9. Super Saiyan is now SUPER SAIYAN DAWN. SSJ2 and SSJ3 are gone; your peak is now
     PERFECTED DAWN.

  PERFECTED DAWN [YOURS, "experimental"]: gives you a warrior's beard, and messier, excited hair.

  THE SAIYAN PRIDE BOOST (how the forms fight) [YOURS]
    - Like Broly: your power level never stops rising. You're pumped full of power, and it
      rises faster the more you're hit.
    - The price: in both Dawn and Perfected Dawn, as your power level rises your defense
      drops (explained as your Saiyan heart getting more and more lost in the battle).

  [SUGGESTED, not decided]
    - Name: we went with "Dawn" (your call). An earlier idea was "Ultra Blood" (Instinct, Ego,
      Blood).
    - The Oozaru (parked) becomes this quest's star, so it needs its import problem fixed
      (it T-posed in Studio).
    - The ki veil as a mini-game: keep your ki inside a band. Too much and it erupts (cracks
      the ground: you fail); too little and the veil collapses. The night is sped up.
    - The tail could be rigged like the hair, so the hair physics moves it.
